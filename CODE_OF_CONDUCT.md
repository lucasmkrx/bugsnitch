# Code of conduct

Welcome participants of every background and experience level. Be constructive, respectful, and clear. Technical disagreement is encouraged; address ideas and evidence without personal attacks.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexual harassment, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Respect privacy and do not share another person's confidential information.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, request a change in behavior, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants for serious or repeated violations. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider context. Concerns may be raised with maintainers through available GitHub contact mechanisms; avoid publicly posting sensitive personal details.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces maintained by Bugsnitch.
