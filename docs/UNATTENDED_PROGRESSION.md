# Unattended ticket progression

**Proposal.** Let an operator authorize one paired machine to work through a bounded set of approved tickets. The runner should start eligible tickets, resume when a blocker clears, and carry a granted action through to its receipt. The team still decides what may ship. Success means more independently verified outcomes per operator intervention under equivalent approval policies.

Zero already derives its board and dispatch from a common decision, resumes eligible tickets, deduplicates retries and caps continuations. Its continuation queues a worker with a standing prompt. BugSnitch can go further by using its typed execution stages and durable receipts to resume a specific operation directly. Waiting for CI, reconciling a push, or recording a deployment observation should consume zero model turns. The Zero snapshot's `plugins/zero-cockpit/city/lib/ticket-next.cjs` and the “One card, one press” section of its architecture guide describe that baseline.

## Implementation

`local-unattended` offers a read-only `observe` command and a durable grant for execution. The CLI and Work board use a common hosted next-action projection. A detached supervisor wakes on committed project events and periodically re-reads state. A restart preserves the grant, turn count and attempted actions. It can reconcile a commit against its saved intent, while ambiguous execution keeps its journal and reservation for inspection. [Runner setup](RUNNER.md#authorize-unattended-local-work)

An operator selects an approved plan on the runner, previews the ticket revisions, and enables unattended execution for a time window. They choose the maximum tickets, concurrent work, model turns, and whether bounded corrections are allowed. Suppose ticket B depends on ticket A. The runner builds and reviews A, then shows the owner a publication decision for the exact candidate and target. It can work on other eligible tickets during that wait. When an authorized person records the accepted integration evidence for A, B starts without another terminal command.

The grant can end at a reviewed local candidate or include an exact publication target. With a target, the supervisor can carry an existing owner approval through publication, monitor the PR, prepare combined validation, and read current deployment evidence. Stewardship is an additional opt-in. It produces a reviewed correction and asks the owner to approve that exact update before pushing. Named plan decisions use **Work**, with the local plan review remaining on the runner.

## Implementation order

| Slice | What to build | Acceptance test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Explain the next action | Derive one typed decision from current facts: act, wait for a named event, ask a named role, or finish. Include the subject revision, reason, and evidence needed to proceed. Surface it in the CLI and Work board. Run in observation mode before enabling execution. | Replay historical attempts. CLI and board agree on the next action, including paused, revoked, stale, offline and uncertain states. Every wait identifies what can clear it. |
| 2. Run a bounded plan on the owning device | Add a separately enabled local supervisor. Persist the operator's grant in SQLite, bound to device, project, plan, ticket revisions, local worker configuration, allowed actions, limits, and expiry. Reuse selection, leases, worker stages, validator, and independent review. A fair scheduler can serve other eligible tickets while one waits, subject to existing priorities and locks. | Close the initiating terminal, exhaust and restore capacity, and restart the supervisor. Each eligible ticket reaches review once. Expiry, a changed plan, pause, or revocation prevents further work under the old grant. |
| 3. Resume safely after events and interruptions | Journal action identity and intent before each side effect. On wake, re-read current authority and reconcile the local journal, worktree and provider receipts. Use events for prompt wake-up and periodic reconciliation for missed events. Carry a retry budget across restarts. | Deliver an event twice, lose a push acknowledgment, and interrupt between commit and journal update. Resume the recorded action or show a specific hold. An uncertain live process keeps its reservation. |
| 4. Carry approved work through delivery | Invoke the existing shipping path when the owner approves the candidate and target. Reuse PR watches and stewardship for changed checks or review feedback. Revalidate and independently review corrected candidates. Prepare combined-validation evidence for sign-off, observe GitHub's merge, and collect the deployed-build verdict where a monitor exists. | Given one publication approval, the runner publishes once. A moved head invalidates its sign-off. A CI transition advances the correct PR without a model turn to rediscover its state. A redeployment requires fresh verification. |
| 5. Ask once and resume | Give ticket-level questions a named recipient, exact subject, expiry, and one recorded answer. Show the proposed action and the consequence of approving it. Existing chat dispatch decisions cover project pause/resume. Add narrowly typed ticket decisions where a remote answer is appropriate. | A valid answer unblocks exactly one matching checkpoint. Stale, duplicate, or out-of-scope answers cannot authorize another revision. A recurring failure with unchanged evidence produces one actionable hold. |

The hosted service remains authoritative for scope, approvals and reservations. SQLite and the worktree on the owning device establish what actually executed. A wake-up triggers a fresh read. Before repeating a billable model call or an external write, the executor must establish the previous outcome or leave the action held. Begin model-backed unattended trials with an explicitly authorized runner-side API credential. Evaluate personal Codex sign-in separately against the project's recorded access constraint. ## Approval boundaries

| The runner can do this under its local grant | Who authorizes the next boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Start eligible, preselected tickets and run registered validation and independent review | Owner or scoped approver approves the execution plan. The local operator enables the device and its limits. |
| Make a bounded correction within the approved scope and repeat validation and review | A material change to requirements, sensitive edit, ambiguous recovery, or exhausted budget goes to the responsible person. |
| Push the reviewed candidate and request a draft PR after approval | Owner authorizes the exact candidate and target. |
| Collect checks, review and combined-validation evidence and prepare the handover | GitHub reviewer approves the PR. Owner or scoped approver signs off on the inspected revision. The owner separately authorizes protected queue delivery where enrolled. |
| Observe the merge and ingest deployment and behavior-check evidence | CI/CD executes deployment. Owner or scoped approver records the handover. A monitored incident needs matching evidence from the active deployment. |

Use the existing work board, local batch runner, journal recovery, PR watcher, shipping flow and merge protections as implementation entry points. ## How to earn the comparison

Instrument the baseline before enabling execution. The current paired trials grade a local candidate. Both products passed the feature and incident trials with equal validator permission. The Zero adapter's unsupported interruption scenario supplies no comparative result. [Existing evaluation](FACTORY_EVAL.md#local-product-probes-november-24-2026)

1. **Build a common event replay.** Exercise an ordinary ticket, an accepted dependency, delayed or failing CI, a duplicate event, a lost publication response, a runner restart, a moved PR head, a revoked approval, and a recurrence on the active build. Specify the correct final milestone or required hold before each run.
2. **Run matched pairs.** Use the same ticket, starting commit, model, validator permissions, time and turn budgets, CI events, and approval responses. Compare ordinary progression from the first authorized start. Measure backlog first-start automation separately. Use an independently graded candidate for the early milestone and an independent probe on the served build for end-to-end delivery.
3. **Measure useful autonomy.** The primary metric is the share of eligible tickets reaching the agreed milestone with zero avoidable human interventions. A required approval counts toward total human time. Clicking Continue after CI clears counts as avoidable. Also report operator-active minutes, p95 time from unblock to action, turns and cost per verified completion including failed runs, and the share of required holds handled correctly.
4. **Set the win condition in advance.** A proposed target is a 15-percentage-point improvement in unattended completion, with a paired 95% confidence interval above zero and no regression in independently graded outcomes. If a separate baseline pilot puts Zero above 85% completion, preselect a 30% reduction in p95 unblock latency at equal or better completion before the head-to-head run. Choose the sample size from the pilot, then freeze the scenarios, metrics and thresholds. Require every scripted safety case to hold correctly, zero duplicate publications, and zero false verified releases. Use scripted replays for fault coverage and matched live trials for the product claim. [Replay harness](FACTORY_EVAL.md#paired-unattended-progression)
